# 0002 — Metal 25T horns + threadlocker

**Context.** Stock MG996R plastic horns stripped on the spline under valve load.

**Decision.** Metal 25T horns, horn screw threadlocked.

**Consequences.** No more slip; horn screw must be serviced with threadlocker in mind.

**Part used.** The [MECCANIXITY metal 25T single-arm servo horn](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G2CFPKC6) (aluminum, split clamp, 28 mm long, 7.5 mm thick, 3 mm hole). Rob modified the printed design so this arm fits; the 3D model in `hardware/print/` fits it exactly. Other metal 25T arms may need the model adjusted.
